# Visual pass 8: the Home style everywhere, with clearer headings

Pass 7's Management Home is now the reference system for every area:
Management, Staff (Coach) and Client (Parent). Navigation, destinations,
content and business rules are unchanged. This is styling only.
- New stylesheet: `css/reference.css`.
- The Home heading changes are in `css/home.css`.
- Screenshots: `docs/screenshots/pass-8/`.

## Headings: clearer and bolder
The condensed serif is now kept to one moment per page: the greeting on each
Home, the Client welcome, and the Management Home feature panels.

Everything structural is Instrument Sans at weight 650–680 with tight
tracking:
- page titles (Needs attention, People, Schedule & Sessions, Finance);
- card headings (Today's schedule, Needs attention, Recent activity);
- chapter headings (Your day, Alfie's week);
- section headings;
- the Next session title.

The effect is confident and quick to scan, closer to automotive and
enterprise labelling, and still premium.

## Tabs
- Labels are 16px semibold, and inactive tabs are darker than before, so
  every option is obviously readable.
- The active tab is a raised white tile with a soft layered shadow and a
  short bronze bar on its left edge. It replaces the thin tick under the
  label.
- The track is a quiet recessed tone, not glass.

## Canvas and surfaces
- Warm porcelain everywhere, with the same faint ridgeline behind each page
  head.
- Operational content sits in large soft cards (22px radius, layered
  shadow, no borders): tables, queues, schedule days, More groups and
  placeholders.
- Side rails (Shortcuts, Your family) are the quieter translucent rail cards
  from the Home.
- Table rows hover with a soft tone rather than lifting.

## Staff and Client shell
- On desktop, the floating top bar is now the same obsidian object as the
  Management sidebar.
- Its active tab has the same champagne fill as the sidebar's active item,
  and the organisation mark matches the organisation card.
- On phones the top bar stays light, with the floating capsule tab bar, so
  small screens stay calm.

## Brand
- The primary action ("Add new") uses the organisation's accent (bronze for
  Relvor, blue for Josh Evans).
- The obsidian shell and champagne active states belong to the Relvor
  platform, so they stay the same across organisations.
- Status colours stay separate from both.
